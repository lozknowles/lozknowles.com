FROM PLAYER
TO PROGRAMMER.

Same curiosity. Bigger playground.

Arcade games were my way into computing. A screen full of moving pixels was an invitation to find out how it all worked.

At 17, I wrote Space Bike for the Commodore 64. It was published alongside ten other Imagineered games. Limited memory made room for imagination.

That thread runs through the things I build today: try an idea, see what happens, then have another go.
