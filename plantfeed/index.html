How recommendations work

PlantFeed is conservative by design. Household leftovers are supplements, not magic fertilisers. Coffee grounds are normally directed to compost rather than straight into pots; banana-water is treated as a mild supplement; eggshell calcium becomes available slowly; unsalted rice water is optional rather than essential.

UK seasonal safeguard: indoor plants generally need much less feeding as growth slows. Never fertilise dry compost or a stressed, newly repotted plant just because leaves look unhappy.

Prototype care guidance, not a soil or laboratory diagnosis. If symptoms persist, check light, watering, drainage, pests and roots before adding nutrients.
