ABOUT

What this demonstration is — and is not

This is an independent prototype built to demonstrate a possible results-day and enrolment workflow. It is not an official Lincoln College service and does not make offers, enrolment decisions or academic judgements.

Design principles

  • OCR can assist data entry, but a person must verify the result.
  • Published grade requirements are evaluated transparently, with each check shown.
  • Non-grade requirements such as interviews, references, portfolios, DBS checks, fitness or placement suitability are never silently treated as passed.
  • Course data is a dated snapshot and each encoded course links to the official source.
  • No uploaded results are transmitted by this static prototype.

Data snapshot

Encoded criteria were checked against public Lincoln College pages on 24 August 2026. The College states that entry requirements differ by course and students should check the specific course page.
