How it works

Motion and tilt choose from a collection of written quips. There’s no camera, microphone, account or AI service. This page does not record or upload sensor readings. Your browser provides the speech voices; some may use an online service.

Commentary pauses when you leave the page or lock the screen. It cannot comment while you read in another app. A downward-facing screen could mean you’re reclining or have put the phone down: the jokes are playful guesses.

If motion isn’t available, try opening this page directly in Safari or Chrome. The sample button works without sensor permission. There’s no need to shake hard or drop your phone.
