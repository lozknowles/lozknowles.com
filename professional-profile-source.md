## Current research and applied AI

### Creator and Independent R&D | Agent Control and Tsunora

- Created Agent Control, a governed runtime for AI work, with scoped jobs, provider-neutral execution, authority controls, verification, retained evidence and recovery. Recent work includes context-aware admission, retrieval, checkpointing and bounded repair for local-model tasks.

- Created Tsunora, an experimental digital-work operating environment derived from Agent Control. Its Digital Labour Exchange connects work to qualified workers through admission controls, allocation, outcome verification and accounting; workforce research covers approval pauses, containment and recovery.

- Established an evidence-led approach to evaluation: frozen workloads, retained failures, independent outcome checks and explicit qualification limits. Published Tsunora clean-install qualification records 2,482 passing tests, zero failures and one environment-dependent skip.

- Developed retrieval-based AI work for Ask Collingham, binding generated claims to retrieved source records, applying deterministic validation and presenting provenance. Implemented and verified this claim-bound pipeline in staging.

Tsunora remains experimental research software; production HR/payroll integrations and model-backed workforce execution are separately unqualified. Public work: github.com/lozknowles/agent-control and github.com/lozknowles/tsunora.
