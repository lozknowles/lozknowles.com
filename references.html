What colleagues and clients say

Seven first-hand accounts spanning technology, education, public-sector delivery and business growth.

I worked as a director of MHR with Lawrence over a 12 year period. During that period Lawrence came through the ranks on the technology side of the business and played a fundamental part in the creation of MHR’s next-generation HR and Payroll Solution, now the UK market leading solution known as iTrent. In recognition of his contribution to the realisation, creation and development of iTrent, Lawrence was soon promoted to Managing Director to drive and preside over the rapid expansion of MHR, and the further development of its product and service streams to become the award-winning flexible solution they are today.

During this time I reported directly to Lawrence. A key aspect to the successful growth was Lawrence’s knowledge and understanding of markets, underpinned by his natural drive and impressive appetite for research. There is no doubt that he played a pivotal role in MidlandHR’s success in the public and third sectors by personally educating and supporting the new business sales and business development teams in the detail and nuances of the OJEU framework. It wasn’t unusual for Lawrence to know more about an OJEU bid than the salesperson and bid team that were directly responsible.

Leading the business development teams from the front, Lawrence was instrumental in the growth of MHR in the Education sector, gaining prestigious customers along the way such as Cambridge and Oxford Universities, before expanding the product and service roadmap further into the College market. His knowledge of HR and Payroll requirements, and how products and services might meet the requirements of different market sectors and specific customer nuance, is not only comprehensive but impressive. Further-more his knowledge of, and enthusiasm for, emerging technologies enables him to craft a vision of future potential, to ensure solutions are robust, futureproof and deliver a high level of return on investment.
